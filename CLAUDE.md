# ToolbarKit

A SwiftUI titlebar for macOS that you control. `.titlebar(_:background:separator:content:)` puts any SwiftUI in the window's titlebar. The real close, minimise and zoom buttons stay where macOS puts them. See `README.md` for the why and the API.

## Rules

- **Scope.** The product is a titlebar you fill with your own views. Do not rebuild or restyle the system toolbar. Do not add item machinery, customisation sheets, overflow menus or sidebar tracking unless the maintainer asks for them.
- **Mechanism.** The window keeps a real titlebar with an empty `NSToolbar`, whose style (`.unifiedCompact` or `.unified`) sets the height. That way macOS positions the window buttons itself. The bar's content is SwiftUI in an `NSHostingView`, added as a leading `NSTitlebarAccessoryViewController` and given the modifier's environment. It must stay inside the titlebar: while the app is active the titlebar takes every click in its band, so controls drawn over the band from the content view look right and cannot be clicked. The background and separator are drawn in the band from the content view and ignore hits. Nothing in the window's view hierarchy is moved or looked up by class name. Keep it that way: public API only.
- **Platform and licence.** The public API is SwiftUI only; AppKit is used internally. The minimum is macOS 15. The licence is MIT, and the repository is public, so write clean commits.

## Files

- `Sources/ToolbarKit/Titlebar.swift`: the public modifier, `TitlebarSize`, `TitlebarBackground`, `titlebarTitle()`, and the bar's layout and backgrounds.
- `Sources/ToolbarKit/WindowConfigurator.swift`: configures the window, hosts the bar's content in the titlebar accessory, and measures the titlebar's height.
- `Sources/ToolbarKit/WindowDragArea.swift`: dragging and double-click on empty bar space, plus `TitlebarButtonStyle`.
- `Examples/TitlebarDemo`: the demo app. Its Xcode project is generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml` and is not committed.

## Working on it

```bash
swift build && swift test
Examples/TitlebarDemo/build.sh
open -n Examples/TitlebarDemo/build/TitlebarDemo.app --args -ApplePersistenceIgnoreState YES -size small -appearance dark
```

Things that are easy to get wrong:

- **Launch with `open`.** An app launched directly from a shell can't take focus, so its window looks inactive.
- **Pass `-ApplePersistenceIgnoreState YES`.** A demo killed while in full screen otherwise reopens on a Space you can't see.
- **Keep bar layers off the window buttons.** Anything under the close, minimise and zoom buttons takes their clicks. The drag area starts after the zoom button, and the background ignores hits.
- **Test clicks with real input, in an active app.** Clicks synthesised with `NSWindow.sendEvent`, or in an app that has never been active, pass the titlebar differently and can pass when real clicks fail. Post `CGEvent`s at the HID tap.
- **Check changes yourself first.** Before asking the maintainer to look at something, check it yourself and send a screenshot, captioned with what they should see.
